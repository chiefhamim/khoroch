# Small neighbourhood retail and service businesses (Bangladesh)

Scope: grocery, pharmacy, tea stall, tailor. Research date: 2026-11-17. Anything without a URL is marked "assumption". Many gaps are real; see "Gaps".

## Group-level numbers
- Economic Census 2024 (BBS): about 1.17-1.18 crore economic units; wholesale/retail trade is the largest sector at 40.19% (another report says 41.82% incl. motor vehicle repair). 41.52% of units are household-based. https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/economic-census-2024-report-released-economic-units-grew-nearly-50-in-a-decade/183102 and https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/bangladesh/services-dominate-manufacturing-declines-as-economic-units-jump
- BIDS says the census missed about 3.65 lakh units. https://online91.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/bbs-census-missed-365-lakh-economic-units-bids-4043571
- 86% of units report capital crisis; 34% were denied loans (BBS census). https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1055736
- A ShopUp executive's opinion piece (2023, a vendor, so biased) claims 98% of consumption goes through small retailers and over 95% of retail is disorganised. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/724410

## 1. Grocery / mudi dokan
**Count:** no sourced national count of grocery shops found. Retail trade as a whole is the figure above.
**Sales and margins:** no reliable national figure. One 2020 survey of 100 micro-merchants (68% grocery, four districts, COVID period) found about Tk 657 average daily revenue, so it is not representative. https://innovision-bd.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/COVID-19_Digest-9_Micro-Merchant.pdf Margins: assumption, not sourced.
**Money in/out:** sales are mostly cash plus baki to regular customers. A TBS story (2020) describes a month-end dispute over Tk 2,913 vs Tk 2,000, and says many owners cannot say exactly what debtors owe; excess credit has sunk some shops. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/154465 Outflows (assumption): supplier purchases (often on credit from distributors), rent, electricity, helper wages, and household drawings taken straight from the till.
**Channels:** cash dominant (assumption); bKash/Nagad personal numbers common (assumption); Bangla QR via TallyKhata, which reported about 45,000 active QR merchants in 2024. https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2024/05/30/Bangladesh/SUP/5_02/78bab4010af163b5_02.pdf
**Paper habits:** a paper khata per customer. Halkhata (new ledger opened on Pohela Boishakh, customers invited to clear dues, with sweets) persists in Old Dhaka but is fading elsewhere. https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/143278/is-halkhata-gradually-becoming-a-lost-tradition Cash memo and tally marks: assumption.
**Apps:** TallyKhata is free and works offline, sends an SMS to the customer on each credit sale, and its records help with bank loan applications (2020). https://www.tbsnews.net/node/154465 It claims over a million monthly active users (2024). https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2024/05/30/Bangladesh/SUP/5_02/78bab4010af163b5_02.pdf A third-party mirror shows 4.01 stars from 45.7K ratings (Mar 2026). https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.progoti.tallykhata I could not read the Play Store reviews, so I have no sourced praise or complaints. HishabPati, Hishab and Khatabook-style apps: no sourced information.
**Literacy and phone:** assumption: basic Bangla literacy, Android phone, low-to-mid price.
**Pain points:** disputes over dues; forgetting entries; customers distrusting the owner (all from the TBS story above).
**Top 3 logs (assumption):** (1) credit sale to named customer: pick customer, amount, one tap; (2) baki payment received: tap customer, amount; (3) supplier purchase/due: supplier, amount. Fast input: recent-customer chips and a number pad.
**Personal vs shop money:** assumption: mixed. The app must offer "owner drawing" as a one-tap category.

## 2. Pharmacy
**Count:** DGDA listed 107,592 licensed drug shops (Dec 2017, via a thesis). An estimated equal number are unlicensed; no current official count exists. https://www.scribd.com/document/646814844/Thesis-Report and https://www.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/renewal-of-drug-licence-stands-at-29095-1554811337 A health minister reported 12,592 unlicensed pharmacies. https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/health/12592-unlicensed-pharmacies-country-health-minister-331381 These figures conflict.
**Margins:** no reliable figure. Chemists sought a 25% commission increase from companies. https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/chemists-druggists-demand-25-commission-hike-from-pharmaceutical-companies/170153 Daily sales: not found.
**Inflow/outflow:** company reps visit shops and offer stock on credit or incentives. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7268404 Buying from reps often gives better terms than stated discounts. https://medium.com/jeeon/is-the-pharmaceutical-industry-ready-for-e-commerce-78d6c08affc9 Expiry returns, rent, electricity, staff: assumption.
**Channels, paper habits, literacy, phone, apps:** no sourced data. Assumption: cash plus bKash; paper purchase vouchers and a baki notebook for regular patients.
**Pain points:** credit stock pushing oversupply (PMC source); expired licences (45% in Chattogram, per https://www.tbsnews.net/node/182053); expiry tracking (assumption).
**Top 3 logs (assumption):** (1) day's cash sale total: one number; (2) distributor due/payment: company, amount, due date; (3) patient baki.
**Personal vs shop:** assumption: mixed.

## 3. Tea stall
**Count:** none sourced. Many stalls sit in the household-based unit category (assumption).
**Sales:** anecdotes only. A Chattogram stall sells cups at Tk 5-100 and reportedly takes Tk 4,000-5,000 a day; another grew from 60-70 to 200-250 cups a day. https://adnchronicles.org/?p=300230 A Dhaka vendor said the income barely runs a household (2022). https://d11.thedailystar.net/business/economy/news/tea-prices-rise-dhakas-roadside-stalls-2987081 No margins found.
**Money flow, channels, paper habits, apps, literacy:** no sourced data. Assumption: tiny cash tickets, daily purchases of milk, sugar, gas; baki for regulars on a wall or notebook tally; bKash rare.
**Pain points:** rising input prices (Daily Star source above); baki memory (assumption).
**Top 3 logs (assumption):** (1) end-of-day cash total; (2) daily purchases; (3) regular's baki, by tapping a nickname, with +Tk 10 / +Tk 20 buttons.
**Personal vs shop:** assumption: indistinguishable. A single daily "took home" entry is the realistic split.

## 4. Tailor
**Count:** none sourced.
**Seasonality:** Eid rush. In Rajshahi (March 2026) many shops closed new orders, with "Eid orders closed" notices. https://b.bssnews.net/district/368731 Customers book early in Ramadan; order cutoffs are often around the 15th-20th of Ramadan. https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/tailors-busy-ahead-eid-rajshahi-1374871 Brahmanbaria (pre-pandemic): small shops' Eid orders double to about Tk 1 lakh. https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/not-making-cut-brahmanbaria-tailors-lose-60-orders-pandemic-242734 Weak Eid demand has occurred (2021, 2023). https://businesspostbd.com/news/2023-04-18/tailor-shops-see-no-rush-of-customers-ahead-of-eid-2023-04-18
**Flow, channels, paper habits, apps:** assumption: advance at order, balance at delivery; a numbered order slip and measurement book; cash plus bKash; cloth sometimes supplied by the customer.
**Pain points:** deadline pressure; remembering balances; idle off-season (assumption).
**Top 3 logs (assumption):** (1) new order: customer, item, price, advance, delivery date; (2) balance collected at delivery; (3) fabric/thread/staff cost.
**Personal vs shop:** assumption: mixed.

## Common patterns across the group
- Cash first, informal baki, no separation of owner and business money (assumption, supported in part by the TBS 2020 story above).
- Disputes and forgotten entries drive adoption of digital khata (TBS 2020).
- One-tap customer baki is the key feature: add, receive, SMS reminder.
- Seasonal peaks (Eid, Boishakh) for tailors and halkhata.
- Offline use is needed; TallyKhata already offers it.
