# Salaried and professional earners (Bangladesh)

Status: Phase 1 research. Figures come from web search summaries, not from reading every page in full. Treat numbers as indicative and re-verify before they appear in the product. Anything without a URL is marked "assumption".

## 1. Teachers

**Who and how many.** A DPE-era figure of about 162,000 teachers in about 37,800 government primary schools is from 2014 (old, blog repost): https://moulvibazarsylhat.blogspot.com/2014/08/10-schools-are-funded-by-local.html. TBS reports more than 500,000 teachers in about 39,000 MPO institutions (date unclear): https://www.tbsnews.net/node/898076. No current BANBEIS headcount found (gap).

**Income.**
- Government primary assistant teacher: basic Tk 11,000 (grade 13) and head teacher Tk 11,300 (grade 12), pre-new-scale, per The Daily Star, Oct 2025: https://epfs.thedailystar.net/TDS/2025/10/05/Bangladesh/MAI/5_15/7af75cc21905f614_15.pdf
- MPO assistant teacher: basic Tk 12,500, or Tk 16,000 with B.Ed. House rent for MPO teachers is a flat Tk 1,000, against 45-50 percent of basic for government teachers: https://www.tbsnews.net/node/898076
- Observer puts the average at about Tk 14,500 (MPO) versus about Tk 20,700 (government primary): https://observerbd.com/news/497199
- MPO teachers and staff get a festival bonus of 50 percent of basic: https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/govt-raises-festival-allowance-mpo-listed-teachers-3904301
- Pattern: monthly salary, a bonus about twice a year (assumption: Eid-ul-Fitr and Eid-ul-Adha). Tuition income is irregular and collected monthly per student, mostly cash (assumption). Tuition and coaching supplement pay: https://www.tbsnews.net/node/15497, https://www.tbsnews.net/node/958531

**Money out (assumption).** Rent or home upkeep, children's fees, groceries, parents' support, samity or DPS instalments, medical costs.

**Channels.** Salary arrives through a bank (assumption for MPO; the MPO disbursement route is not verified). Tuition fees are paid in cash or bKash (assumption). Dena-pawna with colleagues and shop baki (assumption).

**Habits and pain points (assumption).** Pocket notebook, a tuition-fee register per student, and SMS. The pain point is that income is not tracked per student, so late and missing payments get forgotten.

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Tuition fee received: per-student template with one-tap "paid this month" (assumption).
2. Household expense: quick-add with preset categories.
3. Salary and bonus credit: parse the bank SMS or log by hand.

## 2. Government employees

**Who and how many.** About 1.15 million were eligible for house-building loans in a 2017 report (old): https://old.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/govt-servants-to-get-upto-tk-75m-as-home-loan-1533095561. No current headcount found (gap).

**Income.** The 9th pay scale keeps 20 grades. Grade 20 starts at Tk 20,000 (was Tk 8,250) and grade 1 is Tk 156,000 (was Tk 78,000): https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/5lee52sqwl and https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/ptkenwfw4n. It is phased in over FY2026-27 and FY2027-28, with grades 10-20 first: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/ninth-pay-scale-to-roll-out-in-3-phases. House-rent rates vary by grade and area, and benefits linked to basic pay are fully in effect from 1 Jan 2028 (bdnews24, 2026): https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/rp2s8qsov2. Pay is monthly. Festival allowance is a percentage of basic (assumption for exact rate; 20 percent for Bengali New Year appears in https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/government-employees-to-get-20-percent-festival-allowances-for-bengali-new-year).

**Loans.** House-building loan for 20 years, up to Tk 6.0 million (flat or building in district towns) or Tk 5.0 million (other areas), rules from 2018: https://old.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/govt-servants-to-get-upto-tk-75m-as-home-loan-1533095561. Repayment pressure is reported: https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/business/local/wtyp0cfrbg. GPF details: not sourced (assumption: monthly deduction, with an occasional advance).

**Money out (assumption).** Rent where there is no quarter, EMI or house loan deduction, children's education, parents' support, utilities.

**Channels.** Bank or EFT salary (assumption), bKash for utilities and mobile top-up, cash for the bazaar.

**Habits and pain points (assumption).** Payslip, bank SMS, and a notebook. Pain: arrears and revised amounts during the phased pay-scale change are hard to reconcile against the payslip.

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Salary credit: SMS parse, split into basic and allowances.
2. Recurring loan EMI and GPF deductions: recurring entries.
3. Bazaar and daily spend: quick-add.

## 3. Freelancers and remote workers

**Who and how many.** A government PDF cites over 1,000,000 registered platform accounts and about 650,000 active freelancers per the ICT Division: https://socialprotection.gov.bd/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bangladeshs-500M-Industry.pdf

**Income.** Irregular USD, assumed per project or milestone (assumption). No sourced BDT averages found (gap).

**Incentive.** The same PDF says export cash incentives of 2.5 percent apply to freelancing earnings, extended to 30 June 2026, which has passed, so check the current status. Sources conflict: Mutual Trust Bank says remote work is not eligible for 2.5 percent but is for 4 percent: https://mutualtrustbank.com/?p=13223. ACE Money Transfer lists IT freelancing as excluded from the wage-remittance incentive: https://acemoneytransfer.com/en/blog/bangladesh-s-2-5-remittance-incentive-2025-guide

**Channels.** Bangladesh Bank cleared PayPal and Payoneer-style services through a bank-intermediary framework (2026): https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/lxga62dv91. A full PayPal launch is unconfirmed; a special committee was formed in March 2026: https://online-d11.thedailystar.net/tech-startup/news/special-committee-formed-examine-paypal-launch-bangladesh-4123726. Payoneer or bank wire, then BDT, then bKash or cash.

**Tax.** The FY2026-27 tax-free threshold is reported as Tk 375,000, but one report says Tk 400,000 (unreconciled): https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/0sug1clq5v

**Pain points (assumption).** Platform fees and the exchange rate on the day, so the BDT received differs from the invoice. Incentive and tax tracking.

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Client payment received: USD, fee, rate and net BDT, with a template per client.
2. Platform and bank fee: auto-compute from the rate.
3. Business expense (internet, coworking): recurring.

## 4. Corporate executives and high earners

**Who and how many.** About 12.8 million TIN holders and 4-4.2 million annual filers (FY2026-27 budget reporting): https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/0sug1clq5v. By 31 Jan 2024, 3.54 million returns had been filed, up 17 percent: https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/latest-annual-individual-return-filing-rises-17pc. Credit cards number about 2.7 million (Feb 2026), with outstanding balance of about Tk 143.65 billion (May 2026): https://observerbd.com/news/590459

**Income.** Monthly salary plus bonus (assumption). No sourced typical amounts (gap).

**Tax and savings.** Investment rebate ceiling falls from 15 to 10 percent, with the investment cap at Tk 7.5 lakh (verify): https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/415588/how-new-changes-to-savings-certificates-will and https://www.pwc.com/bd/en/assets/pdfs/budget/finance-act-2026-key-amendments.pdf. DPS stays eligible. Sanchayapatra interest rates: no reliable current source (gap).

**Money out (assumption).** Rent or flat and car EMI, card bills, school fees, DPS/FDR, share purchases, staff wages (driver, maid) in cash and bKash, parents' support.

**Habits and pain points (assumption).** Bank SMS and card statements, Excel. Pain: scattered accounts, and tax-time proof of investments.

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Card spend: SMS parse.
2. Monthly EMI, DPS and rent: recurring.
3. Staff wage and family transfer: template.

## Common patterns across the group

- Monthly salary credit is the anchor. All four groups need salary SMS parsing and recurring entries (assumption).
- Fixed obligations (EMI, DPS, rent) are better as recurring entries than as one-off logs (assumption).
- A second income stream (tuition, freelance USD) is irregular and needs a per-payer template.
- Tax and incentive rules changed in 2026, so rates must be editable, not hard-coded.
- Do not build on any figure here that is marked a gap.
