# Rural, seasonal and remittance-dependent households (Bangladesh)

Research for Khoroch Phase 1. Facts carry a source URL and a year. Anything without one is marked "assumption". Search-snippet level verification only; figures should be re-checked against the primary BBS/BB reports before they go into product copy.

## 1. Farmers (smallholders, sharecroppers, crop and livestock households)

**Who and how many.** The preliminary Agriculture Census 2019 counts about 16.5 million farm households, 53.82 percent of all households (down from 56.74 percent in 2008) ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/agriculture/191531/bangladesh-houses-16.5-million-farmer-families), [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/views/smallholders-need-intensive-care-1609340481)). About 6.8 million cultivate other people's land, and over 4 million farmers are landless ([same Financial Express piece](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/views/smallholders-need-intensive-care-1609340481)). The 2019 smallholder share is unpublished; the 2008 figure was about 84 percent of holdings (same source). Sharecropping was 39 percent of tenancy in 2008 (same source).

**Income pattern.** HIES 2022 puts average rural household income at Tk 26,163 per month and agriculture at 16.6 percent of household income nationally ([Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/household-income-doubles-6-years-so-does-spending-3497661), [asianews summary](https://asianews.network/?p=126940)). That is a monthly average. The actual flow is lumpy and tied to harvests. Windows are roughly Aus Mar-Aug, Aman Jun-Jan, Boro Nov-May ([encyclopedia.pub](https://encyclopedia.pub/entry/53937), weak source). The real cash cycle is assumption: big inflow at sale, near-zero between.

**Money in.** Paddy sales at harvest. Distress selling is common because loans and household bills fall due then, and only big farmers can store ([Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/reviews/farmers-in-a-price-trap-a-year-long-phenomenon-1568645656)). Prices fell Tk 100-150 per maund in the 2025 Aman peak ([Daily Star, Dec 2025](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/farmers-count-losses-aman-prices-fall-4049136)). Intermediaries include faria, bepari, paiker and aratdar (same Financial Express piece). Livestock, fish and day labour as side income: assumption.

**Money out.** Seed, fertiliser, irrigation and labour. Input retailers often sell on credit, which limits quality choice ([iFarmer blog](https://ifarmer.asia/blogs/35), advocacy source). Tenant rent split, household food and school fees: assumption.

**Debt and channels.** HIES 2022: 39.35 percent of rural families were in debt; average loan Tk 70,506 ([bdnews24/BIDS report, Oct 2026](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/m7xqnypocz)). The same BIDS-based report says NGOs and microfinance institutions give about three in four rural loans, 42 percent of rural households borrow, 41 percent spend over 40 percent of income on instalments, and the effective NGO rate is about 27 percent. MFI loans typically run 40-50 weekly instalments ([MicroSave/Financial Diaries](https://www.microsave.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/THE_DAILY_COSTS_OF_DEBT_PROBLEMATISING_REPAYMENT.pdf)). Bangladesh Bank set a record agricultural credit target of Tk 39,000 crore for FY2025-26, with overdues rising ([Dhaka Tribune op-ed, May 2026](https://www.dhakatribune.com/opinion/op-ed/410074/financing-agriculture-the-farmer-is-not-the)). Cash dominates; bKash/Nagad cash-out via agent and baki at the dealer or grocer are assumption.

**Paper habits, literacy, phone, who keeps accounts.** No reliable source found. Assumption: memory plus a small notebook kept by the literate household member, often a son or the farmer himself; dealers hold the baki khata; NGO passbooks record instalments. Phone: GSMA 2025 gives men's mobile ownership in Bangladesh at 68 percent and women's mobile internet use at 26 percent ([Dawn](https://www.dawn.com/news/1911146/8m-women-come-online-in-2024-as-digital-gender-divide-narrows)). Basic or low-end Android is an assumption.

**Pain points.** Selling low to repay; opaque dealer credit; stacked NGO instalments; no season-level profit view (assumption).

**Top 3 logs and fastest input.**
1. Input purchase on credit (seed, fertiliser, labour). One-tap category chip, amount keypad, "baki" toggle plus dealer name picked from a list.
2. Crop sale (maund, rate, buyer). Preset crop buttons, two numbers, total auto-calculated.
3. Weekly NGO instalment paid. A recurring reminder with a one-tap "paid" confirmation.

All input methods are assumption (design proposals).

## 2. Remittance-receiving families

**Who and how many.** BMET-based reports show about 1.13 million workers went abroad in 2025, up about 11.5 percent, roughly two-thirds to Saudi Arabia ([Bangladesh Monitor](https://bangladeshmonitor.com.bd/en/around-113-million-bangladeshis-fly-abroad-for-jobs-in-2025), [Financial Express/RMMRU](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/bangladeshs-overseas-labour-migration-up-12pc-in-2025-rmmru)). Departures reportedly fell about 39 percent in the first six months of the new government ([bdnews24, Oct 2026](https://bdnews24.com/diaspora/e5dfaf80f9fa)). The count of recipient households is not found.

**Income pattern.** Calendar 2025 inflows were a record of about $32.8 billion, up 22 percent ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1324231)). Typical per-household monthly amounts: no reliable source found. Assumption: monthly, sometimes irregular, spikes before Eid.

**Money in.** The 2.5 percent government incentive, with bank and exchange house rates leaving recipients around Tk 125 per dollar, comparable to hundi ([TBS summary](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1324231)). Mobile financial service remittances grew more than 90 percent in 2025. bKash received remittances into 4.1 million accounts and about Tk 20,000 crore ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/corporates/bkash-accounts-receive-tk200bn-remittances-2025-1346176), [Daily Star](https://online91.thedailystar.net/business/organisation-news/news/bkash-receives-tk-20000cr-remittances-expatriates-2025-4093911)). Nagad figures: not found.

**Money out and savings.** One 303-household survey found most remittance goes to land, house construction or repair, and ornaments ([Green Univ. article](https://siteadmin.green.edu.bd/uploads/documents/journal-article/2_1700192983_article-document.pdf), small sample). Daily household spending, DPS and samity deposits: assumption.

**Migration cost and loans.** Transparency International minimums: about Tk 68,000 for Saudi Arabia, Tk 100,000 for Malaysia or the UAE. Actual Malaysia costs reported at Tk 500,000-800,000 ([Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/NYU3qQDrJyU2Ewbx/)). A TBS study found workers paid three to six times the fixed cost ([TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/migration/false-promises-abroad-no-justice-home-new-study-sheds-light-migrants-perils)). Repayment is typically from remittance (assumption); sources are land sale, informal lenders and NGOs (assumption).

**Who keeps accounts.** Studies find left-behind wives gain spending authority ([2020 study via IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/jda/journl/vol.54year2020issue2pp127-144.html); [RMMRU 2025](https://www.rmmru.org/newsite/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/WP-54.pdf)). Effects vary by destination ([AEA 2024](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2024/program/paper/a4Z7b3h2)). Paper habits and literacy: no source found; the family likely reports to the migrant by phone call (assumption).

**Pain points.** The migrant wants to see where money went; loan repayment tracking; irregular amounts (all assumption).

**Top 3 logs and fastest input.**
1. Remittance received (amount, channel, rate). Notification or SMS parse, else a two-field form with a channel chip.
2. Migration-loan instalment paid. A saved loan with a one-tap payment.
3. Household spend and land/DPS/gold saving. A category chip with a keypad; an optional shareable monthly summary for the migrant.

Input methods are assumption.

## Common patterns across the group

- Income is lumpy, so the app needs period views (season, remittance cycle), not only monthly ones (HIES monthly averages hide this).
- Debt is central. Rural households borrow heavily and NGO instalments are weekly ([BIDS report](https://bdnews24.com/bangladesh/m7xqnypocz)). Baki, dena-pawna and loans need first-class treatment.
- Mobile money is rising fast for remittance (see above), but cash is likely the main channel for daily spending (assumption).
- Low-end phones, limited literacy and women's lower internet use ([Dawn/GSMA](https://www.dawn.com/news/1911146/8m-women-come-online-in-2024-as-digital-gender-divide-narrows)) imply Bangla-first, icon-led, few-tap flows (assumption).
