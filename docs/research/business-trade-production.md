# Business segment: trade, transport and production

Research date: 2026-11-17. Figures come from web search summaries; several were not opened at the primary source. "Assumption" means unsourced. Source dates are given where known.

## Group context
- BBS Economic Census 2024: about 1.17 crore economic units, +50% since 2013, slowest growth since 1996. https://unb.com.bd/category/Bangladesh/economic-census-2024-report-released-economic-units-grew-nearly-50-in-a-decade/183102
- Manufacturing units reported at 1,041,468 (2024) vs 902,583 (2013). Share reported as 8.77% (preliminary) or 9.57% (later report), so figures conflict. https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/bangladesh/services-dominate-manufacturing-declines-as-economic-units-jump
- Wholesale and retail trade is the largest category (about 40-42% of units, reports differ). Same FE link above.
- Census headline: 86% of units report a capital crisis and 34% were denied loans. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1055736
- No independent survey of khata, Excel or app adoption was found. Vendor claims only: TallyKhata, Hishabee, Hisabplus. https://www.tallykhata.com/en/home-eng/ , https://hisabplus.com/Blog/accounting-software-for-small-business-bangladesh

## 1. Wholesalers (paikari / arat)
**Size.** No count of arat or paikari businesses was found. Trade as a whole is the largest census category (see above).

**Money size.** No sourced figure. Assumption: single credit deals of lakhs to crores of BDT, with the baki balance larger than cash on hand.

**Money in and out.** Assumption: in as part-cash, part-cheque and part-credit from retailers, with commission (arat) or margin (paikari). Out as supplier payments, labour, transport, and shop rent.

**Channels.**
- Cheque. Dishonour is criminal under Negotiable Instruments Act s.138 (up to one year's jail or a fine up to 3x the cheque amount). A cheque must be presented within six months. https://mahbub-law.com/dishonour-of-cheque-law-in-bangladesh , https://lawhelpbd.com/special-law/cheque-dishonour-case/
- Party default risk is real. A Khatunganj trader allegedly defrauded traders of Tk 100 crore. https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/khatunganj-trader-flees-after-allegedly-defrauding-businesses-tk100cr-906616
- Assumption: cash, bank transfer, bKash/Nagad and dena-pawna by mutual trust.

**Bookkeeping.** No sourced data. Assumption: a handwritten party khata or a bound ledger per party, plus a daily cash-book. Assumption: some use Tally or Excel through an accountant.

**Literacy and phone.** Assumption: literate owners, a mid-range Android phone, and staff who keep the book.

**Pain points.** Cheque deadlines, party balances disputed from memory, and Eid-time cash crunch (assumption).

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Sale on baki to a party (pick the party, then the amount; voice or number pad).
2. Collection from a party, by cash or cheque (party, amount, cheque date).
3. Payment to a supplier or commission.

**Personal/business mix.** Assumption: the shop till doubles as the household wallet. The owner draws cash without recording it. A "withdrawal" tag is the key feature.

## 2. Transport operators
**Size.** BRTA registered about 6.7 million vehicles in total, 5.0 million of them motorcycles (source date unclear; a 2025 report gives about 6.4 million). Trucks were 150,676 and buses 54,687 as of 31 Mar 2024, per BRTA data cited in the press. These come from search summaries of mixed dates. https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/accidents/422655/road-safety-day-deaths-continue-as-111 , https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1033676

**Money size.** A Satkhira to Chattogram round trip burns about 230 litres of diesel. A fuel rise of Tk 20 per litre therefore adds about Tk 4,600 (reported). https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/rkycz0u6vw

**Money out.**
- Tolls. At least Tk 1,000 per trip on the Chattogram-Dhaka route (association claim). https://www.tbsnews.net/node/449946
- Monthly extortion of Tk 2,000 per truck (reported). https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/extortion-via-monthly-contract-issuing-cards
- Fuel, driver and helper pay, maintenance, and instalments. Many buyers struggle with EMI, and an association official says nearly 50% of trucks are idle. https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/big-drop-in-commercial-vehicle-sales
- Daily deposit from drivers to owners. A home ministry report notes it pushes drivers to overcharge. https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/transport/damning-home-ministry-report-uncovers-fare-irregularities-338806

**Money in.** Assumption: trip freight paid in cash by the party or through a broker, some on credit. Rental vehicles pay the owner a daily joma.

**Bookkeeping.** No sourced data. Assumption: a pocket notebook per vehicle or trip slip, and the driver's verbal account.

**Pain points.** Unrecorded small payments (tolls, tips), fuel price swings, and EMI against irregular income.

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Fuel fill (litres and amount).
2. Trip freight received or daily joma.
3. Toll, tip and small repair spend (quick-tap categories).

**Personal/business mix.** Assumption: the owner's household pays out of the same cash as the vehicle's running costs. One owner often has 1 to 3 vehicles, so a "per vehicle" tag is needed.

## 3. Online sellers (f-commerce)
**Size.** e-CAB's general secretary has cited over half a million small social-media traders. https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/trade/selling-products-facebook-page-you-will-need-licence-soon-219142 A Financial Express article gives about 50,000 Facebook outlets, so counts conflict. https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/views/e-commerce-in-bangladesh-where-are-we-headed-1578666791

**Money in.**
- Cash on delivery dominates. Estimates are 80-90% (a 2026 vendor blog) and over 90% (trade.gov). https://www.egrow.com/en/blog/bangladesh-cod-whatsapp-2026 , https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/bangladesh-ecommerce
- Courier payouts take 2-10 days, and at best weekly for small sellers. https://futurestartup.com/2022/10/09/cash-on-delivery-still-dominates-ecommerce-payments-instacod-aims-to-change-it-for-online-sellers/
- Some couriers offer instant withdrawal. https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/corporates/ecourier-launches-insta-pay-547262
- Bangladesh Bank reportedly requires banking-channel settlement between couriers and sellers. https://bd.apparelresources.com/?p=195213

**Money out.**
- Ad spend is in USD. Meta ads were limited during the dollar crisis. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/622426
- bKash is now accepted for Meta ads. https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/small-businesses-in-bangladesh-can-now-pay-for-meta-ads-with-bkash
- Courier fee, packaging and stock purchase. Return rate: only an unsourced claim of 20-30% exists, so this is an assumption.

**Bookkeeping.** Assumption: Excel or Google Sheets, or a Messenger order list, matched against the courier portal. Vendor apps exist.

**Pain points.** Cash trapped at the courier, returns that cost twice the delivery fee (assumption), and dollar ad cost with VAT. A 2023 report put the rate at Tk 122-123 per USD including 15% VAT, against Tk 95 earlier. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/622426

**Top 3 logs.**
1. New order with a COD amount (copy the amount from chat).
2. Courier settlement received (bKash or bank).
3. Ad spend, entered in BDT or USD.

**Personal/business mix.** Assumption: very high. The seller uses one personal bKash for customer payments, ads and groceries. A "business" flag per wallet transaction is critical.

## 4. Small manufacturing
**Size.** 1.04 million manufacturing units in the census (see Group context). Many are micro units. Manufacturing employs about 9.03 million in non-farm work. https://en.prothomalo.com/corporate/local/y77wyaacbx

**Money in and out.** Assumption: advances on orders, balance on delivery, and receivables on 15-60 days. Out as raw material (on credit or cash), piece-rate or weekly wages, rent, electricity and gas. Loans come from banks, NGOs or samity (assumption, no figures). 34% of units were refused loans (census, above).

**Channels.** Assumption: cash for wages, bKash for small suppliers, cheque for larger buyers.

**Bookkeeping.** No sourced data. Assumption: a notebook for wages and a separate order register.

**Pain points.** Uneven cash flow, receivables chasing, and wage day arriving before payments do (assumption). Capital shortage is widespread. https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1055736

**Top 3 logs.**
1. Raw material purchase (supplier, amount, paid or baki).
2. Order advance received and balance due.
3. Weekly wages by worker (piece count times rate).

**Personal/business mix.** Assumption: the workshop is often at the home or shares its rent. Family labour is unpaid and unrecorded.

## Common patterns across the group
- Baki (credit both ways) is central, for parties, couriers, and receivables. The app needs a per-party running balance.
- Money mixes between owner and business. Tag transactions as business, personal or withdrawal from day one.
- Cash dominates, with bKash as the small-value channel. Cheque matters only in wholesale and manufacturing.
- Fast capture is a number pad, a party picker and templates. Voice is optional (assumption).
- Per-unit tags (per vehicle, per order, per party) matter more than categories.
- All 4 groups lack independent bookkeeping-habit data. Treat the habit rows above as hypotheses to verify in user interviews.
