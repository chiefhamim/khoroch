Design Philosophy

Inspired by Copilot Money & Wise: puts the primary question ("How much is safe to spend today?") at 40px at the top of the Hero Card, clusters actions into a 4-pill thumb dock, and merges Spending + Top Categories into one card.

7-Slide Onboarding Philosophy

  • Scenario Cards (Slide 2): Choose Calendar Month (1st) vs Salary Day (5th/10th/25th) in 1 tap.
  • Linked Daily ↔ Monthly Math (Slide 3): Pick either your Monthly Cap or your Daily Safe Target.
  • 2-Column Bento Grid (Slide 4): Tactile category cards with real Bangladeshi examples.

Focused Wallet Carousel (Slide 6)

Instead of stacking multiple multi-field forms on one page, Slide 6 uses a single focused wallet card + keypad with a Next wallet → button so you can set up Cash, bKash, and Card in 6 seconds flat.
