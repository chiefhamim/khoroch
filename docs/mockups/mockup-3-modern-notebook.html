Design Philosophy

Inspired by Things 3 & Revolut's flat architectural ledger: treats LenDen as a crisp, structured personal money register (হিসাবের খাতা) with chapter pills, tabular ledger rows, and an in-Hero mode toggle.

7-Slide Ledger Onboarding

  • Slide 1 (Daybook Preview): Shows a live Daybook page (Started with ৳6,500 · Out ৳420 · Left now ৳6,080).
  • Slide 3 (Envelope Breakdown): Breaks your Monthly Budget into Monthly, Weekly, and Daily envelopes automatically.
  • Slide 6 (1-Screen Register): All 6 wallet presets fit in one clean checklist register with inline balances.

Homepage Architecture

  • In-Hero Switcher: Switch Hero focus between Budget Plan and Wallet Net while keeping the 3-metric footer visible.
  • Liquidity Proportion Bar: Visualizes Cash vs bKash vs Card Due at the top of the Wallets card.
