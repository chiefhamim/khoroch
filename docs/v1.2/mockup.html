Budget App v1.2: money notebook, refined

Same structure as the first v1.2 draft (wallets, dues, ledger, themes, sharing), plus the seven day-to-day features. Changes from the first draft:
  • The balance card is one solid colour. No gradient, no glow ring, no glass tiles, no shadow.
  • Category icons sit on a neutral tile and carry the colour themselves, instead of the rainbow of tinted squares every finance app has.
  • Wallets are a plain list with real balances, not a sideways carousel of cards.
  • Fewer pills. Filters are underlined words, and details on the Add screen are rows you tap.
  • The tab bar sits flat at the bottom with a hairline, not floating.
  • No masked card numbers, no three-dot menus, no ellipsis anywhere (AGENTS.md 4b).
