Khata খাতা

Every fintech app is a gradient card on a grey background, because they all copy the same banking apps. Nobody in Bangladesh keeps money in a gradient card. They keep it in the red cloth-bound khata: ruled pages, a red margin line, ink entries, a "carried forward" at the bottom of every page. This direction takes that, not as a costume, but as the actual structure: one page per day, written top to bottom, closed at night.

  • Paper, ink, marginTimes live in the margin, like a shopkeeper's. Out is brick-red ink, in is green ink. No glow, no glass.
  • Real typeNewsreader for headings, IBM Plex for writing, Plex Mono for numbers that line up, Hind Siliguri so ৳ and Bangla are drawn properly.
  • Your handwritingMargin notes ("after Jummah") are shown in a hand font. They are the human part of a ledger.
  • Stamps, not badgesPAID, SETTLED and SHORT are rubber stamps, slightly crooked, because that is what they are.
  • Day-to-day featuresCount the cash at night, a bazar list that becomes one entry, the month's bills, polite reminders for people who owe you, payday envelopes, a budget for a wedding.
  • Every action is a written wordNo trailing dots, no hidden overflow menus, no broken separators. If you can do it, it says so.
