Budget App v1.2 "money notebook" direction

This is a visual reference for docs/v1.2/DESIGN.md. The app becomes a personal money notebook: what you have (wallets like bKash, Nagad, Cash and Bank), what moved (in, out, transfers), who owes what (dues), and whether you're on plan (budget).
